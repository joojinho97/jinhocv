About

I am a Ph.D. student in the M.S.–Ph.D. integrated program in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Yonsei University. I work on AI-driven medical image reconstruction and generative models.

My research focuses on accelerated MRI reconstruction and rapid quantitative MRI (qMRI) mapping, especially scan-specific and self-supervised methods that do not need fully sampled training data. My work has appeared at CVPR, MICCAI, and ISMRM.

  • Accelerated MRI
  • Parallel imaging
  • Quantitative MRI
  • Zero-shot / self-supervised learning
  • Generative models
